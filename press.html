About the publisher

Gjemo AS is a small independent games studio based in Norway. We make games that respect the player: real craft, no ads and no tracking.

About the game

Snooker Duels is a snooker game for iPhone. It follows the full professional rules, has an opponent that plays safety and tactics like a pro, and presents every frame with TV-style camerawork. Beginners get on-screen help; experienced players can switch it all off.
